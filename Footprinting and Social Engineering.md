**Footprinting :**

* process of gathering information about a target organization, system, or network before launching an attack or security assessment.
* first phase of ethical hacking 
* used to identify attack surfaces, technologies, network infrastructure, employee information, and potential vulnerabilities.
* **Passive footprinting**: where information is collected from public sources like WHOIS, Google, and social media.
* **Active Footprinting:** Direct interaction with target systems. eg. ping scan, port scan, traceroute, DNS queries.
* **Prevention Methodologies:** information restriction, security awareness, DNS hardening, firewalls, and monitoring reconnaissance activities.



**Social Engineering :** 

* a psychological manipulation attack 
* where attackers trick individuals into revealing confidential information or performing actions that compromise security.
* attackers exploit human behavior such as trust, fear, curiosity, or urgency.
* **Common attacks:** phishing, spear phishing, vishing, smishing, baiting, impersonation, and tailgating.
* **Potential Risks:** credential theft, unauthorized access, malware infections, and data breaches.
* **Prevention Methodologies:** employee awareness training, MFA, verification procedures, phishing detection, and strong security policies.
