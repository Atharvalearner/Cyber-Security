**# Spyware:**

malicious or unwanted software 

designed to secretly monitor a user's activities and collect information without the user's informed consent.

***The main purpose is: Surveillance and Data Theft***



**# It may collect:**

login credentials

browsing activity

keystrokes

screenshots

clipboard data

system information

financial information
