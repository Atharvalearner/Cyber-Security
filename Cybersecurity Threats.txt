1. Snooping: Unauthorized access to information.
	    - using tools like sniffers and wiretappers. 
	    - It breaks confidentiality

2. Modification or Alteration: Altering data.
	    - like Man-In-The-Middle Attack
	    - It breaks confidentiality and Integrity. 

3. Masquerading or Spoofing or Repudiation of origin: Mimics the identity.
	    - By mimicking identity of someone attacker can generate and distribute attack.
	    - It breaks Integrity 

4. Delay and Denial of Service: Crash the server by flooding and consuming all resources.
	    - It breaks Availability.
