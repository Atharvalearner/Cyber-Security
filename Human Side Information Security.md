* Focuses on how human behavior affects the security of information systems. 
* Organizations implement technical controls such as firewalls, encryption, and IDS/IPS, many security incidents occur due to human errors, lack of awareness, insider threats, or social engineering attacks.
* Common human-related threats: phishing, weak passwords, tailgating, shoulder surfing, and insider misuse. 
* Attackers often exploit trust, fear, curiosity, or urgency to manipulate users.
* Organizations address these risks through security awareness training, strong access controls, MFA, security policies, monitoring, and a culture of security awareness.
