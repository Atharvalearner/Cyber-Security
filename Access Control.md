* a security mechanism 
* used to manage and restrict who can access systems, applications, resources, or data, and what actions they are authorized to perform.



* It works by:

  1. **Authentication**: Verifies: Who are you?

&#x09;		Example: username/password, MFA

&#x20;  2. **Authorization:** Determines: What are you allowed to do?



* **Access Control:** Mechanism enforcing authorization policies.



* Important for protecting sensitive data, enforcing least privilege, preventing unauthorized access, and reducing security risks.



* Implement using authentication systems, IAM policies, MFA, authorization checks, auditing, and monitoring mechanisms.
