Phishing is a social engineering attack in which an attacker pretends to be a trusted entity, such as a bank or company, to trick users into revealing sensitive information (passwords, bank details, OTPs) or installing malware.





**# Types:** 

1\. Email Phishing : Fake emails pretending to be from trusted companies.
Example: “Your bank account will be locked! Click here to update password.”



2. Spear Phishing : Targeted phishing → aimed at a specific person/org.
Example: CEO receives a fake invoice email crafted just for them.

3. Whaling : Spear phishing aimed at high-profile targets (CEOs, executives).
Example: Fake legal notice email sent to a CFO.

4. Smishing (SMS Phishing) : Fake text messages.

5. Vishing (Voice Phishing) : Fake phone calls pretending to be bank/tech support.





**# Prevention phishing:**

organizations should conduct user awareness training, implement multi-factor authentication, deploy email security solutions, verify website URLs, use DNS or web filtering, and keep systems updated. Users should never share passwords or OTPs and should verify the authenticity of emails and websites before entering sensitive information.
