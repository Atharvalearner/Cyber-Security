**Denial of Service (DoS):**

* cyber attack in which an attacker overwhelms a system, server, application, or network resource with excessive requests or malicious traffic, making it unavailable to legitimate users.
* Attackers Goal is: Availability disruption by exhausting resources such as bandwidth, CPU, memory, or connections.
* Single attacking system, So One source IP.
* Easier to detect and block.



**Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS):**

* a large-scale DoS attack
* where multiple compromised systems, often part of a botnet, simultaneously flood a target with malicious traffic to exhaust resources and disrupt services.
* Thousands of source IPs
* Harder to detect and block



***Botnet :*** A network of compromised devices remotely controlled by an attacker to perform malicious activities such as DDoS attacks.



**Types of DoS/DDoS Attacks:** SYN flood attacks, UDP floods, Ping/ICMP floods, and HTTP floods, Smurf attack, etc.



**Prevention methods:** include firewalls, rate limiting, IDS/IPS systems, load balancing, SYN cookies, and DDoS protection services.
