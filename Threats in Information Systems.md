***Threats to Information Systems :***

* potential dangers or malicious activities that can exploit vulnerabilities and negatively impact the confidentiality, integrity, or availability (CIA) of systems and data.
* It may be natural, human, environmental, or technical in nature. 
* eg. malware, phishing, ransomware, insider threats, DDoS attacks, password attacks, SQL injection, and social engineering.
* **It Cause:** data breaches, financial losses, service disruption, and reputational damage.
* **Prevention:** firewalls, IDS/IPS, encryption, MFA, patch management, backups, monitoring, and employee awareness training.
